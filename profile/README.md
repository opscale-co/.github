## Why we built this: Personal reason

It’s fun to code—we get it! But let’s take it a step further: let’s create a business with that.

We’ve all been there—spending 5 hours coding to automate a 5-minute task or kicking off yet another pet project while leaving 10 others unfinished. It’s part of the journey, right? 😅 But here’s the thing: good developers automate things, while great developers systematize first, then automate. Without a solid system, automation can turn into chaos.

This is why we built this: to bring your ideas closer to reality with a battle-tested backbone of best practices. By abstracting the core features of dashboards into reusable packages, we save ourselves (and you) time, effort, and a lot of frustration. It’s about working smarter, not harder—because let’s face it, we’ve all got better things to do than code another CRUD with authentication from scratch.

## Why we built this: Business reason

We were profoundly inspired by the concept from the book Atomic Habits:

> “You do not rise to the level of your goals, you fall to the level of your systems.”

Systems are the foundation of success, especially for companies striving to scale. Whether you’re launching a startup and building an MVP or managing an established business and defining processes for your team, one thing remains constant: creating an effective system is a critical step.

Dashboards serve as the backbone for systematizing a company’s operations, providing better control, enhanced visibility, and actionable insights. They allow businesses to monitor performance, optimize processes, and align teams around shared goals.

We built this solution to make creating such systems more efficient, reliable, and scalable. By abstracting key dashboard features into reusable packages, we empower companies to focus on what matters most—growing their business—while giving them the tools to systematize and scale with confidence.

## Why we built this: Technical reason

We faced the same challenges across all our projects, both internal and external. Over time, we realized the importance of adapting the UX to align with the day-to-day activities of people. Additionally, products with large databases and deployed with Laravel Vapor presented significant challenges due to the limitations of Lambdas. These experiences motivated us to create **reusable, UX-focused and fine-tuned packages**. By doing this, we not only streamline our own development process but also empower the community to use these tools for their own projects.
